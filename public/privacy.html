Privacy Policy

bryf teleprompter — last updated 9 September 2026

bryf teleprompter is a teleprompter that runs entirely in your browser. It has no backend, no accounts and no analytics. This policy describes what the app touches and what it does not.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no server to send data to. We do not collect personal information, we do not use cookies or tracking pixels, and we run no analytics, advertising or third-party telemetry.

Where your script lives

Your script text and your prompter settings — colours, text size, margins, speed, toolbar position — are stored in your browser's localStorage on the device you are using. They never leave the device. Clearing your browser's site data for this domain deletes them permanently, and we hold no copy.

Files you import from your device

A .docx file opened through the device file picker is read and converted to text in the browser. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Google Drive import

Importing from Google Drive is optional and only happens when you tap that option and sign in with Google. When you do:

  • The app requests a single OAuth scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This is per-file access: the app can only read the file you explicitly select in Google's own file picker. It cannot list, browse or read anything else in your Drive.
  • The selected document is downloaded directly from Google to your browser and converted to prompter text. Google Docs are exported to .docx by Google on the way in.
  • The resulting text is stored in localStorage on your device, exactly like a script you typed yourself.
  • The Google access token is held in memory for the duration of the page session only. It is never written to storage and never sent to us. It is discarded when you close or reload the page.
  • We store no Google account information — no name, no email address, no profile data, no file list.

Limited use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained from Google Drive is used solely to display the document you chose inside the teleprompter on your own device. It is not transferred to any third party, not used for advertising, and not read by any human.

Third parties

The app loads Google's sign-in and file picker scripts from Google's own servers, and only after you tap the Google Drive option. A build with no Google credentials configured never contacts Google at all. Beyond that, the app makes no third-party requests.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date above.
